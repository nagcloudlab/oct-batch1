# Information Security Policy

## Passwords and Authentication
All employee accounts must use a password of at least 14 characters and multi-factor authentication. Hardware security keys are mandatory for anyone with production access to the Nimbus Fleet Console.

## Robot Data
Zephyr robots never record or store video. Depth camera frames are processed on the robot and discarded within 200 milliseconds. Only anonymised maps and telemetry are uploaded to the Nimbus cloud, and they are stored in data centres in Frankfurt.

## Incident Reporting
Any suspected security incident must be reported to security@nimbus-robotics.example within 1 hour of discovery. The security team runs a post-incident review within 5 business days.

## Laptops
Company laptops must use full-disk encryption and auto-lock after 5 minutes of inactivity. Lost or stolen laptops must be reported within 1 hour.
