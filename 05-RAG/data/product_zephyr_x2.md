# Product Guide — Zephyr X2 Cleaning Robot

## Overview
The Zephyr X2 is Nimbus Robotics' flagship autonomous floor-cleaning robot, launched in March 2024. It scrubs, vacuums and dries hard floors in a single pass and is designed for hospitals, airports and shopping malls.

## Battery and Charging
The Zephyr X2 uses a 48V lithium iron phosphate (LiFePO4) battery. A full charge gives up to 14 hours of continuous cleaning in eco mode, or 9 hours in turbo mode. Charging from 0% to 100% takes 3.5 hours on the standard dock, or 90 minutes on the FastDock accessory. The battery is rated for 3,000 charge cycles.

## Cleaning Performance
The robot covers up to 1,800 square metres per hour. The clean-water tank holds 40 litres and the dirty-water tank holds 45 litres. Noise level is 54 dB in eco mode, which is quieter than a normal conversation, making it suitable for night shifts in hospital wards.

## Navigation and Safety
Zephyr X2 navigates with two 3D LiDAR sensors, six depth cameras and a bumper ring. It stops within 15 centimetres when a person steps in front of it. Maximum speed is 1.2 metres per second. It can climb ramps with a slope of up to 8 degrees.

## Pricing
The list price of the Zephyr X2 is 28,500 euros. A Robot-as-a-Service subscription is also available for 950 euros per month, which includes maintenance and software updates.
