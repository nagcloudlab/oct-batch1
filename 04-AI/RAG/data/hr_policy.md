# Employee Handbook — HR Policies

## Paid Time Off
Full-time employees receive 27 days of paid time off per calendar year, in addition to public holidays. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year. Carried-over days expire on March 31.

## Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with their manager's approval. Engineers working on hardware prototypes are expected in the lab at least 3 days per week. Fully remote arrangements require approval from the COO.

## Parental Leave
Primary caregivers receive 26 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks of fully paid leave. Leave can be taken within the first 12 months after birth or adoption.

## Learning Budget
Every employee has an annual learning budget of 1,500 euros for courses, books and conferences. Unused learning budget does not carry over.

## Onboarding
New employees complete a two-week onboarding program. In the first week every new hire, including non-engineers, operates a Zephyr robot for a full cleaning shift.
