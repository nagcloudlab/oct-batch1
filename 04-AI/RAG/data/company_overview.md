# Nimbus Robotics — Company Overview

## History
Nimbus Robotics was founded in 2017 in Pune, India by Asha Varma and Daniel Okafor. The company started in a rented garage building warehouse inventory drones. In 2020 it pivoted to autonomous floor-cleaning robots for hospitals and airports. Nimbus opened its second office in Rotterdam, Netherlands in 2022.

## Mission
Our mission is "clean spaces, zero effort". We build autonomous cleaning robots that are safe around people, quiet enough for hospitals at night, and simple enough to be operated without training.

## Leadership
Asha Varma is the Chief Executive Officer. Daniel Okafor is the Chief Technology Officer. Priya Raman joined as Chief Operating Officer in 2023 and leads manufacturing and customer support.

## Numbers
As of 2025, Nimbus Robotics has 340 employees across Pune and Rotterdam. More than 4,800 robots are deployed in 21 countries. The largest single customer is the Changi Airport cleaning contractor, which operates 212 Nimbus robots.
