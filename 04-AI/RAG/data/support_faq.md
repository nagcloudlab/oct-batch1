# Customer Support FAQ — Zephyr X2

## How do I contact support?
Email support@nimbus-robotics.example or call the 24/7 hotline. Priority 1 tickets (robot fully down) get a response within 2 hours. Priority 2 tickets get a response within 1 business day.

## What does error code E-42 mean?
Error E-42 means "LiDAR occlusion": one of the LiDAR sensors is blocked or dirty. Wipe both LiDAR domes with a dry microfibre cloth and restart the robot. If E-42 appears again within 24 hours, open a Priority 2 ticket.

## What does error code E-17 mean?
Error E-17 means the dirty-water tank is full or the float sensor is stuck. Empty the dirty-water tank and rinse the float sensor with clean water.

## What does error code E-88 mean?
Error E-88 is a battery management system fault. Do not attempt to charge the robot. Power it off and open a Priority 1 ticket immediately.

## What is the warranty?
Every Zephyr X2 includes a 2-year limited warranty covering parts and labour. The battery is covered for 3 years or 3,000 cycles, whichever comes first. Damage from using non-approved cleaning chemicals is not covered.

## How do I update the software?
Software updates are delivered over the air every month. Updates install automatically between 2 AM and 4 AM local time while the robot is docked. Administrators can postpone an update by up to 14 days from the Nimbus Fleet Console.
